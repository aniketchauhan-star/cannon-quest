CANNON QUEST: THE VOICE SCRIPT: UPDATE ONLY
11 lines, about 50 seconds to read

Every line in the game is said in one real person's voice: yours, or a teammate's. No AI voice.
These are only the update's lines (every line of the game: docs/VO_RECORD.txt).
The same person who reads the rest reads these too, so the game keeps one voice.

HOW TO RECORD
- One person reads all the lines, in their own natural voice.
- A quiet room with soft things in it (curtains, a bed, a sofa). Not a bathroom or an empty room:
  they echo. Fan and AC off, door shut, phone on silent.
- Use the phone's voice recorder (Voice Memos on iPhone, Recorder on Android).
  Hold it about a hand-span (15-20 cm) from your mouth, a little below it, and keep it still.
- Read the lines below in this order, in one go. Wait about 2 seconds after each line.
- Stumbled? Pause, then say the WHOLE line again. The last take of a line is the one used.
- Say x as the letter: "ex".
- Talk to the player like a friend, not like reading. Smile on the happy lines: it can be heard.
- Send the file as it is (AirDrop, Google Drive, or WhatsApp as a DOCUMENT).
  Not as a WhatsApp voice note: that squeezes the sound.
- Then put it in the game folder, in recordings/, and say which file it is.

THE LINES

 1. Here’s the equation.
    (first game: the spotlight is on the equation; calm and clear, like showing a friend)

 2. This is your cannon.
    (first game: the spotlight is on the cannon; calm and clear, like showing a friend)

 3. These are your orbs.
    (first game: the spotlight is on the orbs in the tray; calm and clear, like showing a friend)

 4. Tap the −5 orb to free x!
    (first game: a hand taps the -5 orb; once tapped it flies into the cannon; calm and clear, like showing a friend; say −5 as "minus five")

 5. Another x is trapped. Free it!
    (a new equation comes; excited, inviting)

 6. Brilliant shot!
    (the shot worked: x jumps out of the cage; big and happy: celebrate!)

 7. Oh no! x is still trapped.
    (the shot missed: x is still in the cage (a retry comes next); gentle and kind, never like "wrong")

 8. Not quite! Try again.
    (the shot missed: the same equation comes back for one more try (no hint: the player works it out again); warm and encouraging)

 9. Watch how it’s done.
    (the second try missed too: the game shows the way, firing the right orbs itself; calm and clear, like showing a friend)

10. Amazing! You freed x every time!
    (the end: the player freed x every time; big and happy: celebrate!)

11. Great job! You freed x in most rounds.
    (the end: the player freed x most times; big and happy: celebrate!)
